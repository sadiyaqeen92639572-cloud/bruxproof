Frequently Asked Questions

Is Remi a good night guard brand?

Yes, with a caveat. In our test the guard performed very well (3.8% thickness loss) but our sample measured 2.60mm vs the advertised max of 2.0mm. This may vary by batch. For heavy grinders this extra thickness is actually a bonus, but those who need thin guards should verify upon receipt.

Does Remi come with two night guards?

Yes. Remi ships two guards per order (upper and lower) by default, which is better value than competitors who charge extra for the second guard. This is a significant advantage if you prefer to alternate or have a backup.
