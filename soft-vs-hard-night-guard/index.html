Soft vs Hard Night Guard

Material Differences

Soft night guards are typically made of EVA (ethylene vinyl acetate) and are comfortable but can actually encourage more chewing in some heavy grinders. Hard acrylic guards are rigid and highly durable, making them the standard recommendation for severe bruxism to protect the temporomandibular joint (TMJ) and teeth from extreme pressure [1].
