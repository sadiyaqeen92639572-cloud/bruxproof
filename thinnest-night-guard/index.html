Frequently Asked Questions

What is the thinnest custom night guard?

The Pro Teeth Guard Hard Acrylic measured 1.55mm in our tests — the thinnest custom guard we found, and it still only lost 3.2% of its thickness after 30 nights of heavy grinding. That's exceptional for such a thin profile.

Can a thin night guard still protect your teeth?

Yes. Hardness matters more than thickness for protection. The Pro Teeth Guard's hard acrylic material at 1.55mm outperforms bulkier stock OTC guards (2.50–2.80mm) despite being far thinner. Thin hard acrylic distributes force better than thick soft EVA.
