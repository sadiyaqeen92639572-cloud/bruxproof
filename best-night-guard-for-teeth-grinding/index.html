Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best night guard for severe teeth grinding?

In our caliper-measured tests, custom lab-made hard acrylic guards like Pro Teeth Guard and JS Dental Lab showed the least thickness loss (under 4%) after 30 nights, making them ideal for severe bruxism.

Are OTC boil-and-bite guards effective?

Boil-and-bite guards like Doctor's NightGuard offer good protection and are a cost-effective starting point, but they typically wear down faster than custom dual-laminate or hard acrylic guards.

How long does a night guard last?

Based on our tests: OTC stock guards (Plackers) can bite through in under 30 nights. Boil-and-bite guards last 1–4 months for heavy grinders. Custom lab-made hard acrylic guards typically last 1–3 years.

Is a custom night guard worth it?

Yes, for moderate to heavy grinders. Our data shows custom guards (Pro Teeth Guard at $190, JS Dental Lab at $170) have only 2.7–3.2% thickness loss after 30 nights vs 8–32% for OTC options. They pay for themselves within 6 months vs replacing cheap guards constantly.
