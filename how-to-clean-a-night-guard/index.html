How to Clean a Night Guard

Best Cleaning Practices

Rinse your night guard with cold or warm water immediately after removal. Brush it lightly with a soft toothbrush (without abrasive toothpaste). For deep cleaning, use non-abrasive denture cleaning tablets or a mild soap [1]. Never use hot water, as it can warp the plastic.
