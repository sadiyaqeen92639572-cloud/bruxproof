Night Guard vs Mouthguard

Function and Design

A sports mouthguard is thick, bulky, and designed to absorb sudden impact to the teeth and gums. A night guard is made of rigid or semi-rigid plastic to withstand sustained grinding pressure over hours and to protect the enamel without disrupting your natural bite [1]. Wearing a sports mouthguard to sleep can cause jaw pain or shift your bite.
