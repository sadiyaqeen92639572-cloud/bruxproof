How Long Do Night Guards Last?

Average Lifespan of Different Night Guards

While dentists often quote 2 to 5 years for custom acrylic night guards, over-the-counter boil-and-bite models generally last between 1 and 6 months depending on the severity of your grinding [1]. Soft EVA guards tend to wear out the fastest, as heavy bruxers can chew through them in a matter of weeks.

Frequently Asked Questions

When should I replace my night guard?

You should replace your night guard if it has visible holes, deep indentations that affect your bite, cracks, or if it no longer fits securely on your teeth.
