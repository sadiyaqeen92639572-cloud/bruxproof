What Causes Bruxism?

The Origins of Teeth Grinding

Bruxism is a multifactorial condition. While historical theories blamed a misaligned bite (malocclusion), modern consensus points to central nervous system factors. High stress, anxiety, sleep apnea, and certain medications (like SSRIs) are strong correlating factors in adult populations [1] [2].
