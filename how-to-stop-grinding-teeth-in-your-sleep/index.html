How to Stop Grinding Teeth in Your Sleep

Managing Sleep Bruxism

Learning how to stop grinding teeth in sleep starts with a hard truth: stopping sleep bruxism entirely is difficult because it is centrally regulated by the nervous system. However, managing it often involves a combination of stress reduction techniques, improving sleep hygiene, avoiding alcohol and caffeine before bed, and wearing a protective occlusal splint to prevent dental wear [1].
